## Starfall 1.0.0 (Minecraft 1.21.1, Fabric)

First release.

- Added Star Shard, crafted from an Amethyst Shard and Glowstone Dust.
- Added Star Block: light level 15, stays bright at night, needs a pickaxe to drop.
- Added Star Cookie: 4 hunger, always edible, 30 seconds of Night Vision.
- Added Blink Wand: blink up to 8 blocks where you look, never into a block, steps up low ledges, fizzles with no cost when there's no room. 64 uses, 1.5 second recharge.
- Custom particles and sounds for blinking, seen and heard by nearby players.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese.

Requires Fabric Loader 0.16.0+ and Fabric API. Install on both the server and the clients.
